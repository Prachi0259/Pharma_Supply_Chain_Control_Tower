# Project Story

### Business question
Which medicines are at risk of stockout, where and why, and how can replenishment improve availability without creating excess inventory?

### Diagnosis
The portfolio had a **93.84% fill rate** and **7.70% stockout rate**. Central DC showed the weakest regional service performance.

### Root cause investigation
Drug-location analysis showed that several Central DC combinations were persistently below safety stock. Supplier analysis showed high PO quantity fill but materially higher delivery lateness, pointing attention toward replenishment timing and inventory positioning rather than simply supplier quantity availability.

### Inventory risk
Expiry analysis identified **₹7.63 Cr of recorded expiry-value exposure**. Inventory imbalance analysis also surfaced potential cross-DC redistribution opportunities for selected drugs.

### Segmentation
ABC-XYZ segmentation identified high-value drugs and showed low demand variability across the portfolio, allowing the analysis to focus on inventory positioning and replenishment execution.

### Scenario analysis
A 95% planning ROP scenario was created using both demand and lead-time variability. This provides a decision-support input for replenishment planning while preserving the distinction between analysis and actual policy implementation.

### Business impact
The project transforms transaction-level supply-chain data into a prioritized view of service failures, inventory risk and replenishment actions. It gives decision-makers a structured way to target interventions rather than applying a blanket inventory increase.
