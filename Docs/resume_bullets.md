# Resume Bullets

- **Diagnosed** a **7.70% stockout rate** across **45,625 sales and inventory records**, isolating **Central DC** as the primary service hotspot with **10.60% stockouts and 104,875 unfulfilled units**; identified inventory gaps and replenishment delays to support targeted availability improvements.

- **Uncovered inventory and replenishment gaps** across **25 drugs, 5 distribution centers and 1,200 purchase orders**, combining **root-cause analysis, ABC-XYZ segmentation, supplier performance, expiry risk and lead-time variability** to identify **₹7.63 Cr expiry exposure** and generate **95% scenario-based reorder points** for inventory planning.
